# Publication-Ready Figure Legends (SCI Manuscript)

**Figure 1 | PRISMA 2020 flow diagram of literature identification, screening, eligibility assessment, and four-dimensional standardization.**
The analysed export contains 456 English-language Web of Science records (articles and reviews; publication years 2000–2026; exported 31 October 2026). Three retracted records were removed (453 non-retracted records retained as a sensitivity file) and 91 further records failed eligibility: 46 with no alveolar-echinococcosis expression in the title, abstract or author keywords, 31 veterinary or preclinical records, and 14 exclusively extrahepatic records. The strict corpus has 362 records: 302 articles (83.4%) and 60 reviews (16.6%), 10,361 citations (mean 28.62 per paper), an h-index of 43, and 6,238 distinct cited-reference strings. The topic-search string is not stored in the plain-text export.

**Figure 2 | Global annual publication output, three-stage evolutionary trajectory, and citation impact in HAE surgical research from 2000 to October 31, 2026.**
**a**, Annual number of publications (bars, left axis) and cumulative publication count (teal line, right axis). Means are arithmetic: 6.0 papers/year in 2000–2009 (60 papers), 11.6 in 2010–2018 (104 papers) and 24.8 in 2019–2026 (198 papers). The 2026 bar is a partial year ending on the 31 October 2026 export date. **b**, Sum of Web of Science Times Cited for papers published in that year (bars, left axis) and mean citations per paper (line, right axis). The year totals are 772 (2003, 12 papers), 1,955 (2010, of which 1,617 are Brunetti et al.) and 1,145 (2019, of which 918 are Wen et al.). Source data: `source_data/fig2_annual_trends.csv`.

**Figure 3 | Geographic and institutional distribution and international collaboration networks in HAE surgical research ($n = 362$).**
**a**, Top 10 most productive countries/regions ranked by publication volume, annotated with total publications and mean citations per paper (CPP). **b**, Country co-authorship network among the top 12 productive countries; node size is proportional to publication output, edge thickness reflects collaboration frequency, and node colors denote regional clusters (orange: China; teal: European endemic/research core; amber: North America, Middle East, and East Asia). **c**, Top 10 most productive institutions after affiliative unification (orange: Chinese clinical centers; teal: European academic centers). **d**, Institutional collaboration network illustrating the dual-core structure anchored by Western Chinese high-volume surgical centers (Xinjiang Medical University, Sichuan University West China Hospital, and Qinghai University) and European reference centers (University of Franche-Comté/CHU Besançon, Ulm University, and University of Bern). Source data are provided in `fig3_countries_institutions.csv`.

**Figure 4 | Prolific authors, longitudinal scholarly output trajectories, core journals, and Bradford's law distribution ($n = 362$).**
**a**, Co-authorship network of the top 12 disambiguated authors, highlighting three major collaborative clusters: the Xinjiang Medical University ELRA and vascular surgery team (orange: Aji T, Wen H, Shao YM, Jiang TM), the West China Hospital and Qinghai hepatobiliary surgery teams (amber: Wang WT, Yang XW, Fan HN), and the European multidisciplinary network (teal: Vuitton DA, Bresson-Hadni S, Gottstein B, Gruener B, Kern P). **b**, Temporal publication and citation trajectories of the top 10 prolific authors from 2000 to 2026; circle size indicates annual publication count and circle color reflects annual citation impact (orange: $\ge 50$ citations; medium blue: $15–49$ citations; light blue: $<15$ citations). **c**, Top 10 core journals ranked by publication count and annotated with CPP (orange bars indicate high-impact journals with $\text{CPP} \ge 30$). **d**, Bradford partition of 169 journals. Journals are ranked by paper count; Zone 1 is the shortest prefix reaching one third of 362 papers (16 journals, 122 papers), Zone 2 the next prefix reaching two thirds (46 journals, 121 papers), and Zone 3 the remainder (107 journals, 119 papers). Axis ticks are 1, 5, 16, 62 and 169. Source data: `source_data/fig4_authors_journals.csv`.

**Figure 5 | Intellectual foundation and co-citation analysis of landmark references in HAE surgical management ($n = 362$).**
**a**, Co-citation network of the top 12 most frequently co-cited references; node size reflects co-citation frequency, edge width represents co-citation strength within the same bibliography, and node color denotes intellectual clusters (teal: WHO-IWGE guidelines and PNM classification; orange: surgical resection, allogeneic liver transplantation, and ELRA clinical cohorts; blue: natural history, epidemiology, and antiparasitic chemotherapy). **b**, Ranking of the top 12 milestone co-cited references by co-citation frequency within the core dataset. Source data are provided in `fig5_cocitation_landmarks.csv`.

**Figure 6 | Keyword co-occurrence, average publication year, prespecified theme counts, and a Callon diagram of those themes ($n = 362$).**
**a**, Co-occurrence among 12 keywords drawn from author keywords and Keywords Plus after thesaurus mapping. Colour marks membership in a prespecified theme; grey marks a keyword that is in none of them. **b**, Average publication year for 16 terms. Antiparasitic therapy averages 2011.6, albendazole 2013.0, PET/CT 2015.4, hepatectomy 2020.7, ex vivo liver resection and autotransplantation (ELRA) 2021.0, and vascular reconstruction 2021.5. **c**, Papers per phase that carry at least one term from each of six prespecified themes. Generic disease names were left out of every theme. **d**, Callon centrality (10 × sum of external equivalence indices) against Callon density (100 × sum of internal equivalence indices / number of theme terms), using keywords that occur at least three times. Cross-hairs are the medians of these six themes. Quadrant names are labels for that median split, not a clustering result. Source data: `source_data/fig6_keywords_clusters.csv` and `source_data/fig6_theme_callon_metrics.csv`.

**Figure 7 | Windowed log-likelihood bursts for 20 prespecified keywords, and keyword counts for operative and diagnostic groups.**
**a**, For each prespecified keyword, every window of 2–12 years in which the keyword occurs in both the first and the last year was scored by a two-proportion log-likelihood ratio, and the highest-scoring window is shown in crimson. The terms were chosen; they are not the top 20 of an all-keyword ranking, and the scores are not CiteSpace burst weights. The three highest scores are hepatectomy 16.33 (2016–2026), vascular reconstruction 11.90 (2018–2026) and ELRA 11.66 (2018–2026). **b**, Papers per period whose keywords intersect each group. Laparoscopic/robotic terms are separate from percutaneous, ablation and ERCP terms. A cell counts papers, and a paper may appear in more than one row. Source data: `source_data/fig7_keyword_bursts.csv` and `source_data/fig7b_modality_heatmap.csv`.
